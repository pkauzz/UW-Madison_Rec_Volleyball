Expect more people than the board shows. Plenty of players never see this page and just walk on, so a court listing four signups can easily have twelve people on it. Treat the numbers as a floor, not a count.

About this site

This is an independent project built by a UW-Madison student. It is not made, run, endorsed, or sponsored by the University of Wisconsin-Madison or UW Recreation & Wellbeing (Rec Well), and it isn’t an official university service.

Court times are copied from Rec Well’s public schedule and can change without notice. Rec Well’s daily schedules are the official source. If they disagree with this page, go with Rec Well.

Signing up here is a heads-up to other players, not a reservation. It doesn’t hold a court or guarantee you a spot. Rec Well controls court access, and its facility rules apply to everyone who plays.

Your display name and signups are visible to anyone signed in to the board. Your email address isn’t shown to other users.

As with any pickup game, you play at your own risk.
